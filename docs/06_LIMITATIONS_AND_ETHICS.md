# Limitations, ethics and responsible interpretation

## Main limitations

1. **HS–COICOP mapping:** customs commodity classifications and consumer expenditure classifications are not identical.
2. **Unit values:** import value/net mass is composition-sensitive and is not a pure transaction-price index.
3. **Small cross-section:** the panel contains six categories with a long monthly time dimension.
4. **Method changes:** HMRC methodology changes around May 2016 and January 2022 may affect comparability.
5. **2026 ONS issue:** a lower-level classification issue is handled through a dedicated sensitivity exclusion.
6. **Endogeneity:** sourcing structure may respond to shocks; the observational design does not establish causality.
7. **Omitted mechanisms:** contracts, inventory, processing costs, retailer margins, domestic production and substitution are incompletely observed.
8. **RDT level mismatch:** country-level HHI is a proxy for structural dependence, not a direct firm-level measure of bargaining power or contractual dependence.

## Ethics

The study uses public, aggregated secondary data and contains no research participants or personal-level observations. Ethical risk is therefore low, but responsible practice still requires attribution, provenance, transparent transformations and claims that stay within the evidential scope.

## Language rule

Results should be described as **associated with**, **consistent with**, or **insufficient evidence**, not as proof of causal effects.
