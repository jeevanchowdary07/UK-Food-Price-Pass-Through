# Research design

## Aim
Examine how import-supplier concentration shapes the magnitude and timing of import-cost pass-through to UK consumer food prices across selected categories, including whether the relationship changes during major disruption periods.

## Hypotheses
- **H1:** positive category-level import-cost changes are associated with subsequent positive CPI changes.
- **H2:** pass-through is stronger and/or faster at higher supplier-country concentration.
- **H3:** concentration-related amplification is stronger in 2020–2023 than in more stable periods.

## Design
Secondary quantitative longitudinal panel; six food categories; monthly frequency; January 2014–July 2026. The design is deductive and mechanism-testing rather than causal identification.

## Theoretical lens
Resource Dependence Theory motivates concentration as a structural dependence measure. HHI is not treated as a complete operationalisation of firm-level dependence: it does not directly observe contracts, inventories, bargaining power, switching costs or domestic substitution.
