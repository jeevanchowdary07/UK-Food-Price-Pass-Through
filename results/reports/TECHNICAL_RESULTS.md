# Technical results

## RQ1
Cumulative 0–3 month pass-through = **0.125339** (SE 0.032312; 95% CI 0.062008–0.188670; p≈0.000105). H1 is supported as an association.

## RQ2
Cumulative HHI moderation slope = **−0.102688** (95% CI −0.981869–0.776492; p≈0.818925). H2 is not supported. The correct interpretation is insufficient evidence of systematic HHI moderation, not evidence that concentration reduces pass-through.

## RQ3
At median HHI, stable-period cumulative pass-through ≈ **0.053257** and disruption-period pass-through ≈ **0.177080**. Difference = **0.123824** (p≈0.007168). However, incremental HHI moderation during disruption = **0.580920** (p≈0.439816), so the stronger disruption pass-through is not statistically attributable to HHI. H3's concentration-specific mechanism is not supported.

## Category heterogeneity
Meat has the strongest cumulative estimate (≈0.2963), followed by Oils & fats (≈0.1551), Milk, cheese & eggs (≈0.1095), Fruit (≈0.1004), Fish (≈0.0432) and Vegetables (≈0.0308).

## Robustness
Every principal RQ1 sensitivity specification remains positive with a 95% CI above zero. See `docs/05_DIAGNOSTICS_AND_ROBUSTNESS.md`.
